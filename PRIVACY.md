# Privacy policy — Reader's Tasks

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Reader's Tasks (`com.freedomfighter.readerstasks`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It shows and edits the task lists of a CalDAV server you choose. It is free, has no ads, no account with the publisher and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the publisher or to anyone else, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.

## Network use

The network is used only to talk to the CalDAV server whose address, user name and password you enter in the account settings (for example Infomaniak, Nextcloud, Tasks.org Cloud or Radicale). The app reads your task lists from that server and writes back the tasks you add, complete, rename, reorder or delete. Requests go to that server only, over HTTPS; nothing is contacted at any other time and no other party receives your data.

## Data kept on the phone

The server address, your user name and your password are kept in the app's private storage so that you do not have to type them again. A copy of your task lists is cached on the phone so the app opens without waiting for the server. When you export your credentials, the file is written where you choose and offered to the share menu; it is yours to keep or delete. Everything is removed when the app is uninstalled. Android's own backup may copy the settings and the cache to the Google account configured on the phone; that is controlled by the phone's backup settings, not by the app.

## Sharing with other apps

Apps signed with the same key as Reader's Tasks (Reader's Launcher) may read and write the task lists through a content provider protected by a signature permission. No other app can.

## Permissions

The app asks only for internet access, to reach your CalDAV server.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
